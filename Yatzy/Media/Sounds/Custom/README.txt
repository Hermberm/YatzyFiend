Your own sounds for YatzyFiend
==============================

Put a sound file here with one of these names, then restart WoW completely:

  yatzy.ogg   (or yatzy.mp3)   - played when someone rolls a Yatzy
  turn.ogg    (or turn.mp3)    - played when it's your turn
  invite.ogg  (or invite.mp3)  - played when you get a game invitation

Then turn on Settings (title bar) -> "Use my own sound files".

Your file is used instead of the built-in sound. Only put in the sounds you
want to replace: with the setting on, a name that has no file here may stay silent. Keep it short (a few seconds)
and not too loud. WoW only finds new files after a full restart (not /reload).
Only you hear your own sounds; other players hear theirs.

Note: when you update the addon, copy your files back into this folder.
